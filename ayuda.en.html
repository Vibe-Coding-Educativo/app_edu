Repository User Guide

1. Find the right resource

  • Main search: Use the large search bar at the top. Type any term (an author's name, a word in the title, a concept like “gamification”, etc.) and the repository will search across all fields of every card.
  • Detailed filtering: For more precise searches, use the Filters panel.
    • Click the Filters button to show or hide it.
    • Inside, you will find categories like Area of Knowledge, Educational Level, or Resource Type.
    • Click a category's buttons to apply filters. In some categories you can select multiple options at once (e.g., Primary and Secondary).
    • Use the “Clear Filters” button to reset just this panel without affecting the main search.
  • Quick filters from cards: In each resource card, the author name, level, area, and keywords are links. Clicking any of them applies that filter automatically.

2. Manage your preferences and searches

  • Save your favorites:
    • Click the heart icon (♡) in the top-right corner of a card. It turns red (♥) and the resource is saved to your favorites.
    • Favorites are stored locally in your browser.
    • To view only favorites, click the Favorites button (with the heart icon) next to the search bar. Click again to see all resources.
  • Share your searches:
    • After applying filters, click the “Share URL” button. A unique link with your selection will be copied to the clipboard.
  • Clear everything:
    • The “Clear All” button resets the page: it clears the search, disables filters and shows all resources.

3. Understanding a resource card

  • Colored border: Indicates the primary platform used to create the resource (e.g., purple for Gemini, orange for Claude, etc.).
  • Title and author: The resource’s name and its creator.
  • Level and area: Target educational contexts. They are quick filters.
  • Keywords: Tags that describe the resource. They are also quick filters.
  • Visit resource: Main button to open the resource in a new tab.

4. Want to add your own resource?

This is a collaborative project. If you have created a digital educational resource (an app, a website, a game, an assistant…) and want it to appear here, join the Telegram group Vibe Coding Educativo and send its link to the Bot de IA Educativa: it reads the resource, prepares its record and publishes it once you approve it. It must be free of charge, with no paid versions, and its code must have an open licence.

5. Modify or delete a resource

From the same bot, with /misrecursos, you can change or remove yours at any time. If you added them with the old form, the bot will ask for the email address you used and send you a code to check that they are yours.

6. Principles of use and responsibility

These tools should always be used responsibly and in line with ethical principles and appropriate use of digital technologies in education.

Important notice

Some resources in this repository integrate Artificial Intelligence. Therefore, when used by minors, an adult (teacher or legal guardian) should always accompany and supervise them to ensure correct and safe use.

We hope you find useful tools for your classes!
